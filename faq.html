What is the admission process?

You can apply online or visit our campus. After a short assessment test and interview, the admission is finalized based on merit.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes, we provide need-based and merit-based scholarships for students who excel in academics or sports.

Is there a transport facility?

We provide a safe and reliable van service covering most areas of Karachi. Contact the transport desk for routes.

What are the school timings?

Primary classes start at 8:00 AM and end at 1:30 PM. Secondary and Technical courses have separate afternoon slots.
